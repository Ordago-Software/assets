# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-05-26

This Privacy Policy explains how ColorPicker ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use the application. Please read it carefully. By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

## 1. Introduction and contact

ColorPicker is a desktop application developed by Oliver García Robleda. The App runs locally on your device. We do not operate any backend server, do not maintain user accounts, and do not transmit your personal data to us.

For any privacy-related question, please open an issue on the project's public repository or contact the developer at **ordagosoftware.info@gmail.com**.

## 2. What information do we collect and why?

### 2.1. Information stored locally on your device

The App stores the following data **exclusively on your local device**, in the operating system's user configuration directory:

- Color collections and palettes you create
- Application preferences (theme, language, keyboard shortcuts)
- Configuration files

This data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly export or share it.

### 2.2. Information we do not collect

We do **not** collect, store, transmit or process any of the following:

- Personally identifiable information (name, email, address, phone number, date of birth)
- Account credentials or passwords
- Payment information
- Screen content or pixel data (color sampling happens locally; nothing is transmitted)
- Telemetry, crash reports or usage analytics
- IP addresses, device identifiers or browser data
- Location data 
- Marketing preferences

## 3. Data sources and sharing with third parties

We do not share data with any third party because we do not hold any. However, the App integrates with the following third-party services that you may already use independently. These services have their own privacy policies, which you should review.

### 3.1. Steam (Valve Corporation)

If you launch the App through Steam, the App communicates with the Steam client running on your device to provide features such as achievements and in-app collectibles. The following information may be exchanged between the App and your local Steam client:

- Your Steam ID (used only to read and write achievements and inventory tied to your account)
- Achievement progress for this App
- Inventory items associated with this App's Steam catalog

This communication happens between the local Steam client and Valve's servers. We do not receive or store this data. See Valve's privacy policy: <https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/>.

### 3.2. Discord Rich Presence

If you have the Discord desktop client running on your device, the App may send activity information (such as your current action in the App) to your local Discord client via local inter-process communication. Discord then displays this information on your Discord profile according to your own privacy settings inside Discord. We do not receive, read or store any Discord account information. You can disable this behavior at any time by closing Discord or by adjusting your Activity Privacy settings in Discord.

See Discord's privacy policy: <https://discord.com/privacy>.

### 3.3. Data outside our control

Once activity information is transmitted to Steam or Discord, the storage, caching and display of that information are governed by those services. Information such as your Discord Rich Presence status may be visible to other users or cached on their devices, and we have no control over that once transmitted.

## 4. Marketing and advertising

We do not run any marketing campaigns, do not send promotional emails and do not display advertisements inside the App.

## 5. International transfers

We do not operate servers and do not transfer your data anywhere because we do not hold any. Any data flows that may occur do so directly between your device and the third-party services described in section 3, governed by their own policies.

## 6. Retention

Because we do not hold personal data on our side, no retention period applies to us. Locally stored data (collections, preferences) remains on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the App.

## 7. Security

The App stores data locally on your device. Therefore the security of that data depends primarily on the security of your own device and operating system. We recommend keeping your operating system, Steam client and Discord client updated, and following standard security practices on your machine.

We do not transmit personal data over the network ourselves, so there is no transit channel for us to secure.

## 8. Your legal rights under GDPR

Even though we do not hold personal data about you, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent regulations you have the following rights with respect to any personal data that any data controller may hold about you:

- **Right to be informed** about the collection and use of your personal data
- **Right of access** to your personal data
- **Right to rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to restrict processing**
- **Right to data portability** in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Right to withdraw consent** at any time
- **Right not to be subject to automated decision-making**

To exercise any of these rights against us, contact the developer using the channels listed in section 1. We will respond within the legally required timeframe. Because we do not hold personal data, most requests will be answered with confirmation that no data exists on our side.

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and there is no age restriction to use it. Because the App does not collect any personal data from any user, no specific provisions for children's data apply. If you obtained the App through a distribution platform such as Steam, that platform's own age requirements may apply to your account.

## 10. Complaints

If you believe that any handling of your personal data has infringed applicable data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

In Spain, the supervisory authority is the **Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD)**: <https://www.aepd.es>.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, in the third-party services it integrates with, or in applicable law. Updates will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 12. Contacting us

For any privacy-related question, request to exercise a right listed in section 8, or any other concern, please open an issue on the project's public repository or contact the developer at **ordagosoftware.info@gmail.com**.
